Installation Direction

Prepare the subfloor before choosing adhesive

Installation requirements depend on the substrate, perimeter, traffic and selected format. The following guidance is for planning and quotation; the project installer should confirm the final method on site.

1. Check the base

The subfloor should be clean, level, dry, sound and free from loose debris, oil, cracks and hollow areas.

2. Bond rubber rolls

Rubber sheets normally require a compatible two-component polyurethane adhesive. Typical coverage is approximately 1 kg per 4 m² for commonly used products, while actual use varies with the adhesive, trowel and subfloor.

3. Roll and press the sheet

Align the rolls to the layout, press the surface with a suitable roller and pay close attention to seams and trapped air.

4. Install tiles with locks

Use the supplied lock connectors between rubber tiles. Adhesive can be added when required by the room perimeter, subfloor condition, traffic or project preference.

Buyer FAQ

Questions before requesting a quotation

Clear answers help buyers and AI search systems distinguish verified supply facts from generic flooring content.

What is the difference between rubber flooring rolls and rubber gym tiles?

Rolls create a more continuous surface with fewer seams and are commonly selected for larger connected zones. Tiles are easier to handle, arrange and replace locally, and are available in thicker formats for project-specific gym areas.

What sizes and thicknesses are available?

Rubber rolls are normally 1 m wide and 10 m long with 3-12 mm thickness. Composite rubber tiles are available in 500 × 500 mm and 1000 × 1000 mm formats with 15-50 mm thickness.

Do rubber flooring rolls require adhesive?

Yes. Rubber rolls are normally bonded to a clean, flat and dry subfloor with a compatible two-component polyurethane adhesive. Typical coverage is about 1 kg per 4 m², but actual use depends on the adhesive, trowel and substrate.

How are the rubber tiles connected during installation?

The rubber tiles use the supplied lock connector system during installation. Adhesive may also be used depending on the subfloor, perimeter, traffic and customer project requirements.

Can both rolls and tiles be supplied in solid and speckled colors?

Yes. Both formats can be supplied with solid-color or EPDM-speckled surface directions. UMAX currently offers 12 standard color directions and discusses project-specific combinations during sampling.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The normal MOQ is 1 m². Packing, sample freight and delivery are confirmed according to the chosen format, quantity and destination.

What product documents are available?

Selected coloured rubber material used in the flooring has passed specified EU RoHS restricted-substance limits in SGS testing. Applicable documents are checked against the selected product and supplied on request rather than offered as a general public download.

What should I send for an accurate rubber flooring quote?

Send the floor dimensions, intended training area, preferred roll or tile format, thickness direction, color, installation base, order quantity and delivery city or port. Photos or a floor plan help UMAX recommend the format more accurately.
