Start With Four Project Details

A Better Brief Creates a Better Quote

You do not need a finished specification. A floor plan and a few project facts are enough to begin a practical product discussion.

01 · SpaceDimensions, floor plan or usable training area.
02 · UseTraining activities, daily traffic and member capacity.
03 · BrandLogo, colors and customization requirements.
04 · DeliveryDestination country, target date and trade preference.

Project-Specific Terms, Clearly Confirmed.

MOQ, production time, available test documentation, warranty and freight are confirmed according to the selected product, quantity and destination.

Project Flow

One Contact, Six Clear Stages

The final scope can be small or mixed-category. The sequence stays clear so that design, specification and delivery decisions are recorded before production.

01 · Project Brief

Space, buyer type, products, destination and target date.

02 · Recommended Mix

Suitable surface and equipment options for the intended use.

03 · Visual Mockup

Logo, lane graphics and selected color coordination where applicable.

04 · Confirmation

Samples, specifications, quantities, packing and trade terms.

05 · Production & Inspection

Production updates, 100% pre-shipment inspection and a customer review video.

06 · Shipping & Support

Export packing, freight coordination, delivery follow-up and after-sales support.
